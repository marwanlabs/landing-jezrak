# Jizrak / Stronger roots

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation, refined by the user's subsequent choices. Final source: src/day/RootedLanding.tsx and src/day/rooted.css. Route: /.

## Creative brief

1. Vibe. User: "premium, trusted"; "like a story with animation layering"; "Be creative and don't take any inspiration from the current Root landing page." Authored direction: warm editorial photography, generous type and one dimensional centrepiece.
2. Journey. User approved the photographic editorial direction. Authored sequence: visible brand; online and counter sales; roots behind the Store; connected operating day; independent Stores and clear access; invitation to start.
3. Energy. Spacious opening, quiet explanation, one root-reveal crescendo, practical evidence, decisive close.
4. Feeling. Possibility, recognition, relief, clarity, confidence, readiness. Concrete causes below.
5. Signature. User: "the brand name is the root of the plantis so we can take this into the idea and the storytelling of the landing page, our feature." A botanical root system grows beneath a Store; visitors select inventory, orders, purchasing, customers or finance to see what it supports.
6. Range and palette. User explicitly keeps the existing palette. Retain forest, limestone, chalk, sprout and dark-theme tokens. Creative differences come from composition, photography, typography and narrative.
7. World. Distinct scenes, not an uninterrupted camera flight. Physical merchant photography above the surface, explanatory botanical illustration below it.
8. Assets. Existing RootMark and local IBM Plex fonts. One generated merchant still, inspected and optimized to a 270 KB delivery JPEG. Original retained here. All labels remain HTML.

Audience: Arab business owners selling online and at a counter. Product facts: src/content/landing.json. End belief: the work behind a sale stays connected while each Store stays independent. Primary action: Start your business, using configured onboarding. Configured sign-in and demo destinations remain respected; the default demo anchor resolves to the existing /demo route.

## Grammar and score

Merchant daybook: normal-flow destination masthead; photographic opening; quiet selling passage; one bounded sticky botanical centrepiece; operating line and asymmetric evidence; oversized closing brand plate. Bans: continuous camera, progress rail, fake dashboard, invented testimonials/statistics, repeating feature-card grid, pinned crossfade text.

Why the eight established grammars were not used: filmic restricts navigation and hard scene changes; editorial forbids this photographic opening; live surface confuses illustration with product operation; continuous world repeats the previous root structure; poster removes needed imagery; gallery loses the causal story; split stage overcommits to a comparison; cutlist fragments the explanatory centrepiece.

| Beat | Device family | Role |

| Close | Static typographic plate | Resolve and invite action |

The data-sc-act flow attributes mark measurement geometry; the table describes the actual device families. A page-local event-driven hook cleans up observers and listeners on route exit. Engine sources are retained unchanged as skill reference files, not loaded into this React route.
