# Jizrak merchant entry

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation carried forward from the Jizrak project.

## Evidence and authored decisions

1. Vibe: user asks for animated, premium, trusted login and sign-up pages. Retain the existing forest, limestone, IBM Plex and Alexandria identity.
2. Journey: recognize Jizrak, see the merchant workspace promise, enter details, understand the next action. This is account access, not another long marketing journey.
3. Energy: calm opening, small expressive specimen assembly, immediate form interaction.
4. Feeling: familiarity from the existing identity; clarity from short labeled fields; ownership when the sign-up store name appears on its own shop plaque; confidence from explicit, honest submission feedback.
5. Signature: naming the store gives the technical shop specimen its identity. No email or password appears in the illustration.
6. Range: premium-minimal, with a technical product illustration rather than stock photography.
7. Structure: a compact merchant entry desk. The earlier continuous-world preference applies to the homepage, not to obstructing account forms with a scroll sequence.
8. Assets: existing favicon and fonts, native semantic illustration. No generated media, no API spend.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate

New grammar: merchant entry desk. Utility header links home and switches locale/theme; a static form is the dominant action; a contextual specimen occupies the companion pane; mobile places the form before the full specimen. End on an honest form result and a features link. Ban pinning, delayed/hidden fields, endless ambient motion, fabricated testimonials, and artificial scroll distance.

All eight marketing grammars would make access slower or mistake a form for a campaign. This deliberately short working surface adapts scroll-craft's depth, restraint and verification without forcing four scroll acts into authentication.

Differs from connected business on all six dimensions. Differs from feature guide on grammar, nav, first-screen device, sequence, close and signature (ownership imprint versus transaction arithmetic). Shares brand tokens and technical illustration medium, intentionally.

## Feeling curve, peak, then score

Familiarity: branded utility header and stable form.
Clarity: visible labels, password reveal, concise requirements.
Ownership (peak): the supplied store name imprints the specimen in real time and holds.
Confidence: validation explains what to fix; preview submission explicitly says authentication is not connected.

Tell-someone sentence: It's the page where the shop becomes mine as I name it.
Silence: stable form, no moving input targets or pinned blank screens.

| Beat | Device | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Arrive | Natural flow and short specimen entrance | Access stays immediate |
| Orient | Independent plaque/diagram assembly | Depth explains the shop and its connected operations |
| Name | Live semantic text replacement | Ownership peak, no decorative typing delay |
| Continue | 160ms press transition, inline feedback | Honest state indication |

Layer contract: far technical grid holds; connecting lines reveal; shop plaque settles from 14px with a small rotation; foreground branch labels enter at 60ms offsets. Form and supporting text remain stable and fully readable. All motion ends; reduced motion renders the complete diagram without positional movement. Mobile uses a short identity ribbon above the form and the full diagram below.

## Integration boundary

No authentication provider or account API exists in this repository. Forms are explicitly labeled previews; credentials stay in component memory, are never persisted or transmitted, and password fields clear after a valid preview submission. No fake success, OAuth, reset email or account creation. No-JavaScript forms remain disabled and do not submit personal information in URLs. Production destination overrides remain intact.
