## Journey and creative result

Continuous-world grammar was explicitly requested. Its fixed scene and map navigation support the merchant sequence: business, brand, operations, decisions, expansion. The alternatives and layer contract are in DESIGN.md. The interview and delegated decisions are in BRIEF.md; the merchant-first correction supersedes the original consumer-order framing.

Signature: a merchant selects a sample store category; that selection carries into the storefront, order, stock reservation, and ending. The same world opens to related operating records, then gains a second store with an independent ledger.

Feeling assessment: intended curiosity → ownership → clarity → confidence → readiness. First visual review showed competing transition headlines, weakening clarity, and an overlong phone headline. Fixed the copy windows, shortened the opening, and added a visible independent-store unit at the close. Final contact-sheet assessment: calm introduction → tangible brand choice → operational clarity → quieter records → resolved expansion. The peak is intentionally controlled rather than theatrical; operations owns the longest travel (2vh versus 1.2vh for other legs).

## Harness interpretation and limits

The stock shoot.mjs was run on desktop and phone; the initial contact sheets exposed overlapping copy, now corrected. Its film-specific worldflight detector flags dead scroll because these segments contain no video. This is not reported as a green stock harness result. The page-local camera is independently checked through real rendered transforms and distinct screenshot pixels; those checks pass. The generic video-decoder assertions are inapplicable to the native diagram renderer. Reduced motion deliberately keeps the camera static while copy and navigation remain usable.

This is a local review build, not a production deployment. No production URLs, pricing, testimonials, or performance claims were invented. Onboarding and sign-in need owner-configured destinations. No real iPhone, Android device, or manual screen-reader session was tested. Chrome automation does not certify those environments.
