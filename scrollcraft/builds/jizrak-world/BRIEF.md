# Jizrak: the connected store

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation, with interviewed direction.

## User direction and eight brief topics

1. Vibe: user said “premium, trusted” and “Take the user into the journey of our features.” Authored interpretation: precise, calm, dimensional, confident. References: an architectural model's spatial clarity, an order's paper trail, the branching structure in the existing Jizrak mark.
2. Sequence: delegated with “Choose the creative direction for me”. Authored journey: meet the connected store; choose a product; follow one order; understand the customer and records; arrive at room to grow.
3. Energy: authored calm opening, close attention at the storefront, expansion at the order connection, quiet confidence at records, resolved ending.
4. Emotion and memory: authored curve below. The visitor should remember following the same order into the parts of the business that handle it.
5. Signature: authored persistent sample order whose selected quantity carries into the reservation illustration and final journey summary. Scroll expands its connected paths into a store map.
6. Range: premium-minimal, supported by the user's premium/trusted direction. Existing forest identity with silver surfaces. No invented business metrics or testimonials.
7. Structure: user explicitly chose “One continuous world”. One fixed worldflight stage, a route map, windowed semantic copy, one spacer. No scrolling section edges.
8. Assets: repository has Jizrak root mark, local Alexandria and IBM Plex fonts, bilingual approved product copy. No product screenshots or photography found. Use authored vector/HTML diagrams, clearly labelled illustrations, not fabricated product screenshots. No paid generation necessary for this technical world.

## Product evidence

Source: src/content/landing.json, src/apple/candidateContent.ts, src/apple/publicContent.ts, README.md. Jizrak joins bilingual storefront, catalog, checkout, optional POS, inventory, purchasing, fulfilment, customer history, supported financial records, and multi-store operations. Reservation does not consume stock; supported financial and customer records are related, not guaranteed simultaneous. Store ledgers remain independent. Production onboarding/auth/demo destinations are owner-configured; local preview must not claim successful onboarding.

Audience: merchants starting or running an online store, with optional physical sales and future multi-store needs. Intended belief: I can run my store's connected daily work here, and understand what happens after a sale. Primary action uses existing “Start your Business” wording consistently.

## Customer correction

User: “Our customers will be the store owner, not the visitors that will buy a product.” Also requested the “inventor or the stackholder” feel what the store builder will do. Interpret as merchant-first with platform clarity for stakeholders. The storefront purchase is supporting evidence, not the main character. Revised sequence: your business, your brand/storefront, your operations, your decisions, your next store. Signature selection is a merchant's sample brand category, retained in storefront, order, and closing summary. Any sample quantity is a merchant-side illustration, never a consumer checkout CTA.

## Feeling curve, before the score

- Curiosity: a luminous, dimensional store surface sits within a wider connected diagram.
- Ownership: a merchant selects a sample store category and sees the branded storefront illustration respond.
- Clarity (peak): the chosen order opens into inventory, fulfilment, customer and financial paths in the same world.
- Confidence: the related records are legible, with their scope and limitations explained.
- Readiness: the view pulls back to the whole store and leaves a clear next action.

Peak: “I saw my storefront open into the operations behind my business.” It lives at the operations waypoint and gets the longest travel.

Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where your storefront opens into the roots of your business.”

Authored silence: no empty screens. The storefront's close-up is the quieter beat before the connection opens. The final camera holds intentionally after the whole system is in view.
