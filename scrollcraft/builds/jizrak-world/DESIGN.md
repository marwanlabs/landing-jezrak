# Design contract

Grammar: continuous world, explicitly selected by the user. Filmic one-shot removes jump navigation; editorial introduces page edges; live-surface requires actual app chrome; poster lacks product substance; gallery emphasizes options; split-stage implies a comparison; rhythmic cutlist opposes the requested continuity. All seven lose to the explicit continuous-world choice.

Fingerprint: registry has no historical builds. First build clears the gate without comparisons.

Worldflight is rendered as an authored commerce diagram rather than a generated film. Its five engine segments share one persistent scene; page-local camera transforms move through the same geometry. This deliberately avoids video-only seam and decoder requirements. The engine remains unchanged. All substantive labels are real HTML. Decorative SVG illustrates products and routes, not a claimed production UI. Four-device act rules do not apply to this grammar, which explicitly bans acts.

| Waypoint | Weight | Device / spatial change | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Your store | 1.2 | Worldflight establishing view, three independently transformed planes | Curiosity |
| Storefront | 1.2 | Camera approaches merchandise; real quantity control | Recognition |
| One order | 2.0 | Camera reveals branching route, sample reservation computed from selection | Clarity; peak |
| Daily clarity | 1.2 | Camera follows related customer and financial records | Confidence |
| Room to grow | 1.2 | Camera resolves to independent store units; action holds | Readiness |

Total: 7.8 viewport heights including final viewport. No artificial blank dwell.

Layer contract: far grid has least displacement, connector plane lies beneath store units, raised merchant surface is focal subject, near sample-order slip overlaps it and has greatest displacement. Typography stays in a separate clear column. Opening: store in its world. Midpoint: connections widen and carry the selected order. Exit: whole business visible, next action holds. Mobile places type above a separately scaled scene, with fixed bottom waypoint navigation.

Controls: jump to every waypoint; quantity carries across route; browse all documented features and FAQs in keyboard-accessible dialog; locale and theme retained; onboarding uses configured destination or honest local notice. Reduced motion keeps the same story and navigation with static camera transforms. No-JS exposes all copy in reading order.
