# Jizrak features

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Evidence and decisions

- Audience: store owners and stakeholders evaluating the store builder, as specified earlier by the user.
- Vibe: the user's “premium, trusted” direction; authored treatment is a calm merchant field guide in the existing forest/silver identity.
- Journey: authored sequence is brand → catalog and stock → sale and fulfilment → customers and decisions → independent Stores.
- Energy: welcoming opening, quiet preparation, one expanded order demonstration, then measured decision-making and a clear ending.
- Feeling: recognition from a merchant's working day; ownership from brand controls; preparedness from component availability; clarity from following a sale; confidence from records; readiness from a capability index.
- Peak: “I followed one sale and saw exactly when the stock changed.” The selling chapter receives the largest narrative span and the only multi-step demonstration.
- Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where you follow your own sample sale from availability to its business records.”
- Signature: choose online or counter selling, then advance a labelled sample transaction; reservation, consumption, tender and downstream sample financial summary stay consistent.
- Range: premium editorial, with native diagrams and working sample calculations. No invented customer metrics or fake application screenshots.
- Structure: the existing homepage remains the continuous world requested earlier. This new deeper route is an independently navigable field guide with compact detail drawers, explicitly requested by the user. It is not another continuous flight.
- Assets: existing Jizrak icon and fonts, provided feature specification. No paid image generation needed for explanatory product diagrams.
- CTA: “Explore all features” leads to the on-page index. Configured business onboarding is used at the close; otherwise “Return to overview” links to the homepage.
- Authored silence: no empty viewports; quiet means less visual motion, never absent content.

## Grammar and fingerprint gate

Merchant field guide: a normal reading document with a sticky topic ribbon, different explanatory spreads, native expandable reference, and an order experiment in the middle. It forbids fullscreen video, fixed-page copy windows, hidden essential prose, and long pinned spacers. The local transaction illustration may hold alongside its prose, but document reading order always works without script.

This differs from the existing continuous-world row on all six dimensions: field-guide grammar, topic ribbon, compact title/working brand specimen, natural-flow six-part sequence, searchable reference close, and transaction-to-record carry-through. Shared identity is intentional.

Other grammars: filmic and continuous-world hide too much detail; live surface would imply a full product demo; editorial's media-free title excludes the useful brand specimen; poster and cutlist lack room for substance; gallery removes the causal narrative; split stage adds a comparison the brief does not need.

## Score and layer contract

| Beat | Primary device | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Opening / brand | Parallax in a bounded diagram | Brand name, product plane and foreground order slip separate at restrained rates; copy stays still |
| Prepare | Reveal | A product resolves into components and available assemblies |
| Sell (peak) | Local sticky trace + working controls | See stock reservation and consumption at distinct steps; online/POS choice persists |
| Understand | Flow + entry | Customer and financial records, with computed sample result |
| Expand | SVG connector draw | Shared Location connects to separate Store ledgers |
| Resolve | Native searchable disclosure | Small capabilities stay accessible without extending the default story |

Hero layers: background grid (slow), focal brand sheet (moderate), foreground order slip (faster). All are semantic diagram markup or decorative SVG. Independent movement is confined to the illustration; mobile places it below a smaller headline. Reduced motion holds a complete composition. Existing unchanged ScrollCraft engine drives local parallax/reveal; custom React state drives the transaction.
